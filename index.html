How SnapIT Shortener Works

1

Input & Validation

Your URL is validated for security and correct syntax. Protocol schemes (https://) are automatically completed if missing.

2

Base62 Hash / Alias

A random 6-character unique slug is generated using Base62 alphanumeric hashing, or your custom chosen alias is matched and checked for collisions.

3

Instant Redirection

When someone visits a short link with ?r=slug, the app looks up the target destination, tracks the click analytics, and redirects in milliseconds.
